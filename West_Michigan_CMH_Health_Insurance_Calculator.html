Methodology & notes
• Employee annual cost is the 2027 bi-weekly employee cost × 26 pays. Employer HSA contributions (Priority HSA 3500 PH06) are shown as a negative amount and reduce total cost.
• Medical costs assume one $150 office visit per $500 of medical cost (up to 12 visits). On BCBS Traditional Value 2000 182 ($30) and Priority Traditional 2000 Custom ($20), office visits are a copay not subject to the deductible. All other medical costs go to the deductible, then 20% coinsurance up to the coinsurance maximum. On the HSA plans, all medical costs apply to the deductible first.
• Rx: Value 2000 182 – $20 generic, $40 preferred brand, $80 non-preferred brand (specialty is 15%/$200 max preferred and 25%/$300 max non-preferred; modeled at brand copays). Value HSA 3500 202 – after deductible $10 / $80 max / $100 max. Priority Traditional 2000 Custom – $10 / $80 max / $160 max, not subject to deductible. Priority HSA 3500 PH06 – after deductible $10 / $80 max / $160 max. Coinsurance tiers are modeled at the per-fill maximum. On HSA plans, before the deductible is met each fill costs 3x that amount (applied to the deductible).
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage. Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum.
• Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included.
